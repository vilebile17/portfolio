**Nine Round Objects** is my first fully decorated [Geometry Dash](https://store.steampowered.com/app/322170/GeometryDash/) level.
It was built for [Destriv's](https://www.youtube.com/@Destriv) [Auto Creator Contest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YxoL-j3_iVA) and is thus of **auto difficulty**, meaning
that the entire level plays itself; there's no need player input at all :)

As the name and song suggest, the level is massively inspired by [Nine Circles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOdPoU1ncOc) which is an infamous 
**demon level** made back in update [1.9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQkAjNPKxDM) and featured a new flashy effect which was then
shamelessly copied by [many.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OhoKYcsjMJM&list=PLJTLhPLbxybiuoXMMmXJrfjV6pOJzczYW&index=2) For this project, 
I wanted to go for something that plays tribute to the original, but not a blantant rip-off. Therefore the
designs are far more intricate and the effects more emphatic through the use of the new objects we have
at our disposal as of [update 2.2](https://x.com/RobTopGames/status/1737283913666486757)

The largest inspiration for this level however, was [Tride](https://www.youtube.com/@TrideGD). Who is a GD youtuber
who frequently makes videos creating levels. I find them very fun to watch and have picked up _many_ little
tricks and ideas through him. It is almost certain that I would not have made this level without coming across his channel
